Squint

Crystal-clear magnification without ads or clutter.

Designed for readability and optical inspection. Squint combines hardware-accelerated camera zoom with variable flashlight strength, freeze-frame inspection, and real-time accessibility contrast filters.

  • 0.5x – 10x Smooth Zoom — Continuous optical scaling with tap-to-focus and exposure lock.
  • Variable Precision Light — Multi-level torch brightness adjustment to eliminate glare on glossy pill bottles and serial tags.
  • Freeze & 24h Retention — Freeze live frames for steady reading; unpinned captures automatically purge after 24 hours.
  • 100% Offline & Private — Zero network permissions declared. Camera feeds never leave device memory.

Our Standard

Built on Trust, Not Engagement Tricks

We believe mobile software should respect your device, your attention, and your privacy.

Zero Surveillance

None of our apps contain tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or telemetry. What you inspect or calculate remains strictly on your device.

Offline-First Resilience

Our utilities run completely offline without relying on remote cloud services. They work reliably underground, in airplane mode, or anywhere without cell reception.

Zero Bloat, Native Speed

Engineered using native frameworks (SwiftUI on iOS, Jetpack Compose on Android, modern standards on Web). Instant launches, minimal memory footprint, and low battery consumption.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Publisher: JSK META LLC (Commonwealth of Virginia, USA)

1. Our Privacy Commitment

At JSK META LLC ("we", "us", or "our"), privacy is not an afterthought—it is the foundational design requirement for every application we engineer. We develop focused, utilitarian software tools with zero surveillance, zero external data collection, and zero advertising.

This policy applies to all applications published by JSK META LLC, including Squint: Magnifier & Light (across iOS, Android, and Web PWA editions).

2. Zero Data Collection Summary

We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, license, or share any personal data, device identifiers, camera feeds, photos, analytics, or behavioral telemetry.

  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate third-party tracking or behavioral analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Mixpanel).
  • No Advertising SDKs: None of our applications contain advertising frameworks or tracking identifiers (IDFA, AAID).
  • No Account Required: Our apps do not require registration, login, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • 100% Offline by Design: Native applications operate locally on your device without communicating with remote servers.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Our utilities request only the minimum operating system permissions necessary to perform their stated functions.

Camera Access (Squint)

Squint requires camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA / NSCameraUsageDescription) solely to display a live optical magnification viewfinder and toggle the device's hardware LED flashlight.

  • Camera frames are processed in temporary volatile device memory in real time.
  • Frames are never uploaded, recorded without your direction, or transmitted off your device.

Photos & Local Storage

When you choose to capture or freeze a magnified image, the image is stored strictly within the app's local sandbox storage on your device.

  • Automated 24-Hour Retention: Unpinned snapshots stored in the app's local cache are automatically and permanently purged after 24 hours.
  • Protected Images: Only items you explicitly designate as "Protected" remain stored locally until you choose to delete or export them.
  • No Cloud Sync: Automatic OS cloud backups (such as Android Auto-Backup) are explicitly disabled for cached snapshots to prevent unintended transmission.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our applications do not collect any personal data whatsoever, they comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Apple/Google family and student safety guidelines.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce new applications or update functionality, this Privacy Policy will be revised accordingly. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated Effective Date.
